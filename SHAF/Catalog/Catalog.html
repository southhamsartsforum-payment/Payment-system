Your Artwork Catalog

On this page you can register details of your artworks which may be for sale at one of the SHAF exhibitions - Creative Passions, Present Maker, or at the Arts Trail (Market Hall). Enter details and click save. If you return to this page you can edit or delete each artwork, and add more. Artworks registered here will be displayed as a "pick list" in the SHAF payment system for ease of purchase. When completing the form tick the target exhibitions. Also if the availability is limited click limited, and specify the number available. So, for a single original click limited - 1. For 10 prints click limited - 10. Don't click limited if there is no limit, eg greetings cards. The SHAF Payment system will decrement the number each time one is sold, and if all have been sold will flag the item as sold out.
